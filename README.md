# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Project Overview
An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to monitor end-to-end e-commerce sales performance, profitability trends, and customer buying preferences. 

## 📊 Key Metrics & KPIs
* **Sum of Profit:** 37K
* **Sum of Quantity Sold:** 5,615 units
* **Sum of AOV (Average Order Value):** 121K

## 📈 Dashboard Features & Visualizations
* **Monthly Profitability Trend:** Bar chart tracking profit fluctuations across all months (highlighting positive vs. negative profit months).
* **Category Breakdown:** Donut chart showcasing product volume share (**Clothing 63%**, **Electronics 21%**, **Furniture 17%**).
* **Payment Mode Share:** Breakdown of customer payment preferences (**COD 43.74%**, **Debit Card 13.2%**, **Credit Card 11.97%**, **EMI 10.49%**).
* **Sub-Category Profitability:** Horizontal bar chart comparing top sub-categories like Printers, Bookcases, Saree, Accessories, and Tables.
* **Geographic & Customer Analysis:** Performance breakdowns by customer names and state-wise totals.
* **Interactive Slicers:** Dynamic filtering by **Quarter (Qtr 1–4)** and **State**.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
* **Power BI Desktop:** Report layout, visualization, and custom neon dark UI theme.
* **Power Query & DAX:** Data cleaning and metric calculations.
